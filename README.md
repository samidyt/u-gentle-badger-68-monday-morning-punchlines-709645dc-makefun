## The joke

> I don't need a Monday alarm. My dread wakes me up ten minutes early.

## Why this line

- **Setup and turn:** the setup promises a reason you don't need an alarm (a body clock, good sleep) — the turn is that dread does the waking, ten minutes early. The punchline lands on a reversal, not on a complaint.
- **Fresh angle:** no "I hate Mondays", no coffee, no snooze-button cliché — the alarm itself is replaced by a feeling, and dread arriving early is the surprise.
- **Tight:** 68 characters, two beats, nothing after the punchline.
- **Retellable:** a one-sentence story anyone can repeat to a friend the same day.
